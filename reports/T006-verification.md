## Conventions needing review (proposals, not client answers)
1. Strict never-exceed in the double expression can cost one step on a one-ulp tie of a decimal-exact fit (documented test: 47 ticks, step 0.1, budget 249.1 gives 52 steps, 53 fit in decimal); about 19% of contrived exact-fit cases lose a step. A relative tolerance would avoid it but would allow exceeding the budget.
2. Tolerances and bounds: 1e-6 step (ONLY float noise on an exact fit in the rounding of n; no longer used for the min/max grid, see 3b), 1e9 steps (max and cap), 1e15 raw steps, 1e13 ticks.
3. Cap fix after the Codex review of 27dd744 (defect: BUY E=2000 SL=1999, step 0.1, budget 30, cap 0.29999995 gave OK 0.3 above the cap): `cap_steps = floor(x + 4*DBL_EPSILON*max(1, x))`, x = cap/step, the same expression and constant (PS_CAP_ULPS / CAP_ULPS = 4) in MQL5 and Python; the 1e-6 step is no longer used for the cap. Hand cases: step 0.1 raw 0.3 lots: cap 0.29999995 -> 2 steps SKIP_ABOVE_CAP, 0.3 -> 3 OK, 0.30000005 -> 3 OK (BUY and SELL); step 0.01 raw 0.03: 0.0299999 skip / 0.03, 0.0300001 ok; step 0.05 raw 0.15 likewise; step 0.001 raw 5000 lots (5e6 steps): cap 4999.999999999 skip, 5000.0 ok; noise 0.7/0.1, 0.1+0.2 and every k*step cap are not misclassified. Accepted noise: volume_out = n*step can sit a few ulps above an on-grid cap (0.30000000000000004). Sums of 60 accumulated steps can drift past 4 ulp; caps are inputs, not accumulations.
3b. Grid fix after the Codex review of 181be94 (defect: max 0.29999995 / step 0.1 accepted as 3 steps = 0.3 above the maximum; min 0.30000005 accepted = 0.3 below the minimum): VOLUME_NOT_ON_GRID now uses `x = value/step`, `k = floor(x + 0.5)`, accept iff `k >= 1` and `|x - k| <= 4*DBL_EPSILON*max(1, x)` (PS_GRID_ULPS / GRID_ULPS = 4), identical in MQL5 and Python; min first, then max; order vs VOLUME_MAX_BELOW_MIN and STEP_RANGE unchanged. Tests: 12 new table rows (both limits, both sides, BUY and SELL, noise cases 0.3, 0.1+0.2, 0.7 accepted), GridRuleTests (sweep k=1..2999 for steps 0.01, 0.02, 0.05, 0.1, 0.25, 0.001: on-grid accepted, +-5e-7 step rejected), 2 mutants (1e-6 back, 1e-6*max(1,x)) caught by the table, 2 static negative controls. One old test updated on purpose (test_grid_tolerance_boundaries: a 9e-7 step deviation was accepted, now rejected). Python and static checks only; MQL5 UNVERIFIED.
4. A cap off the step grid is compared after flooring (cap_steps) and never used as a volume; a cap below the minimum is valid input and always skips; the broker maximum is reported before the cap when both are exceeded; geometry failure is reported before parameter validation.
4. SKIP results keep diagnostics (steps_rounded, volume_raw, ...) but zero volume and risk.
5. The caller must normalize volume_out to the broker volume digits (n*step carries binary noise, e.g. 3*0.1); the integer steps_rounded is authoritative.
