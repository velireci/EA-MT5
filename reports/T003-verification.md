P2 candidate/level from the state known before the evaluated bar, both contexts, consumed candidate kept as the frozen protecting level, retroactive scan at first derivation. P3 one flip trigger (body close beyond the current protecting level), CONTINUATION and INTERNAL_BREAK events, no flip without a level. P6 reconfirmation (touch counted only on bars opening at or after `detected_at`, at the close of the touching candle; INVALID wins). P7 interrupting transitions. P10 start from NONE only (break before seed, CONFLICT not overwritten by the seed on that bar, NO_EVIDENCE). P14 INTERNAL_SWEEP / SWEEP_WAIT / RECONFIRMED (reason INTERNAL), structural logic superseding; the two entry-block flags are only REPORTED (`H3HoldBlock` for S3, `H3SweepBlock` is the technical proposal); nothing acts on them. P11 stateless fixed-depth rebuild: R (default 1000, never below 3 x lookback), warm-up = lookback, check depth = warm-up, hard cap 5000, replays A and B, flags CONVERGED / SEEDED / LIMITED_BY_HISTORY with a reason and the depths used (R requested, R_eff, B depth, warm-up, check depth); wording "converged at depth X vs Y", never "unchanged by more history"; LIMITED reports UNKNOWN and emits no market event; depth-only differences are RE-DERIVATION. ## 5. Ambiguities that affected the code (each marked `AMBIGUITY n` in `H4Bias.mqh`)
Format: what the code does; the alternative. These are decisions for Codex and the owner, not silent solutions.
1. (revision 7, changed) RECONFIRMED(INTERNAL) FREEZES the level (aligned with P3), at the touch with the level known before the touching candle; SWEEP_WAIT still keeps updating the level (P14 proposal). When the recorded FVG is lost on bar T the level thaws and is re-derived at the end of T, used from T+1; if T touches another valid eligible FVG the level stays frozen at the one known before T. A technical proposal, defined in the task file (P14). Alternatives: re-derive one bar later (revision 6, rejected by Codex); never thaw.
2. Touches are evaluated only while HOLD or SWEEP_WAIT; a touch of another eligible FVG during RECONFIRMED is not remembered; after the recorded FVG is lost the fall-back and a new touch can happen on the same bar.
3. When several eligible FVGs are touched on one bar, the one nearest the bar's close is recorded (T002 ordering). The spec names no reference price.
4. A wick bar produces no CONTINUATION or INTERNAL event; those events and sweeps occur only in ACTIVE and SWEEP_WAIT.
5. (revision 6, changed) The dependency anchor is the minimum over the references (origin reference, level pivots and sweep; for a frozen state the wick if any, the frozen-level pivots and the sweep of an internal wait, origin ignored); "minus the bars that confirm them" is read as the `Left` EXISTING bars before each reference in the bar series (index based, converted back to absolute time); missing bars -> LIMITED_BY_HISTORY with the reason ANCHOR_HISTORY_MISSING. Alternative: the arithmetic `Left x period` (rejected: it can name a time that is not a bar).
6. NONE counts as CONVERGED, and any HOLD or frozen RECONFIRMED is CONVERGED even with a seed origin; SEEDED is only for ACTIVE-like states with a seed origin. The spec lists both flags for a seeded HOLD.
7. The consumer emits only the latest bar's events; a cursor older than the previous bar gives GAP and the caller must re-baseline. Alternative: catch up on missed bars.
8. INTERNAL_SWEEP and INTERNAL_BREAK need a defined protecting level (the interior level exists only with it, P14). P3 says internal events continue for a bias without a level.
9. The A/B event comparison covers events from B's first non-warm-up bar onward, by full text; earlier events are ignored.
10. A seed's level is "defined" unless a bar up to t-1 closed beyond it; bar t closing beyond it still seeds and the retroactive scan then removes the level.
11. Equal-price ties pick the earliest pivot.
12. "The low before it" is the immediately preceding extreme in the list, so a minor swing of the extreme kind inside a leg can bound the interval and define the level.
13. "Latest HH/LL" is the latest swing strictly beyond its predecessor; candidates crossed by a close since their pivot are ignored.
14. The sweep wait counts touches from the close of the sweep bar, with candle A at or after the sweep bar's open.
15. After a flip the new bias has no level and origin BREAK; the level is derived at the end of the flip bar, and that retroactive scan includes the flip bar.
16. The retroactive scan covers the bars after the level pivot up to and including the discovery bar; `detected_at = max(level known, wick known, bias start)`; the event is dated by the discovery bar.
17. CONFLICT is unreachable (see section 6); not a decision.
18. NONE_WICK is recorded for P2 levels only.
19. (revision 6, narrowed) A wick during SWEEP_WAIT starts HOLD with the then-current level frozen (it was not frozen before); a wick during RECONFIRMED meets an already frozen level.
20. (revision 7) A touch while NO protecting level is defined (SWEEP_WAIT, level rejected or missing) is not recorded: no RECONFIRMED, nothing frozen, the wait continues; a later touch with a level can record. Alternative: record it and freeze "no level" (rejected: not trustworthy). Tests: (e) in group 22 with a control. The revision 6 details about clearing the candidates are removed: the candidates are kept so no event is lost on T+1.

## 6. Contradictions and observations in the specification
- CONFLICT cannot occur: with consumed candidates excluded, the up-level lies strictly above the down-level (confirmation bars lie between them), so one close cannot break both. The agent's search of 120 random series found none; this is evidence, not proof. The branch is kept and unit-tested through the pure resolver `H3P10Resolve`. The spec's CONFLICT rule is therefore dead in practice unless the candidate definitions change.
- P2 against "minor swings never become the level": taken literally, "the low before it" lets a minor swing inside a leg bound the interval (ambiguity 12).
- P3 against P14 on freezing (ambiguity 1): resolved in revision 6 in favour of P3 for RECONFIRMED; SWEEP_WAIT remains unfrozen by the P14 proposal.
- Case D ("a break and a seed in opposite directions") is reachable only under the literal "up to t-1" reading with a plateau structure; the test `d_break_vs_seed` with control `d_seed_control` shows it.
- Case B2 ("an FVG formed between the wick and discovery"): in the immediate-discovery variant the wick and discovery are at most two bars apart, so no FVG (A at or after the wick, C = A+2) can complete in between; it is testable only in the late-seed (NONE) variant, which the tests use.

### 9.2 Design decisions (mine; none is a client rule)
1. Rebuild only when an H4 bar closes (live) through the T002 life machine (`H4LifeDecide` / `H4LifeOnCopy`, reused unchanged); the copy is `CopyRates(..., start_pos 1, ...)`, so the forming bar is never read; historical mode copies bars closed by AsOfTime and never rebuilds on new bars.
2. Events: the first reliable evaluation sets the baseline cursor (`H3BaselineCursor`); only `H3ConsumeNew` output is marked NEW (larger, green, `>> NEW`, logged). GAP and REDERIVED re-baseline instead of replaying the bar's events as new. A clear (data not ready, failed copy) resets the baseline, so recovery rebuilds without reporting history as new. Historical mode has no NEW events.
3. A LIMITED_BY_HISTORY / unreliable evaluation draws NO proposal-derived object (consistent with "UNKNOWN, no market event"); the panel gives flag, reason, depths and note. A diagnostic drawing of an unreliable state was not added.
4. The default draws the 12 most recent market events and 6 most recent eligible FVGs (plus the reconfirmation FVG always); non-market events and INVALID FVGs are optional. Defaults are mine.
5. Panel: own class with a background rectangle label, wrapped lines, font size input, four corners computed from the chart size (not the T001 `CStatusPanel`, whose prefix is T001's and which has no background).
6. Object names are derived from stable IDs (level id, FVG id, sanitised event id, absolute time stamps); every redraw first deletes the `velo_t003_` map objects (not the panel) and recreates them.
7. T002 direction history is recomputed per bar from the same swings (`H4DeriveDirection` over `H3KnownCount`), only to show "since" and "previous"; the panel checks it equals the replay's `t2dir` (WARNING line if not).

A difference caused by a rebuild (window slid, depth changed) or by missed bars (the date is unknown) was therefore reported as a market change at the wrong bar.
- FACT (Python model, `tests/t003_reference_test.py` group 25): with a baseline at bar n and the next evaluation at n+3 (bars missed, consumer GAP) the legacy behaviour reports `PHASE ACTIVE -> HOLD` and `T002 MIXED -> BEARISH` at bar n+3 although the wick was bar n+1; with consecutive polling each change is reported once at its own bar. The fixed behaviour logs the GAP case as a re-derivation and reports nothing.
- LIMIT: an end-to-end REDERIVED step in which the bias really differs was NOT found in my synthetic trials (scratch trials, not in the repository: about 2,900 consumer steps over 75 random series gave one REDERIVED step and a second trial over 40 series gave two, all with the bias unchanged). That branch is covered by the decision table and by a unit case with an injected previous snapshot, not by an end-to-end series. The GAP branch is end-to-end.
- FIX: `H3TrackChangesMode(consume_status, prev_valid)` (pure): only a consumer status OK with a previous snapshot reports; REDERIVED, GAP and NOTHING_NEW log `T003 RE-DERIVATION (...) ... NOT reported as a market change`; no snapshot or a baseline stores the snapshot. `ConsumeEvents` records the status (`g_cons_status`). ### 10.4 Analysis with SYNTHETIC series: which class gives AB_STATE_DIFF+AB_EVENT_DIFF+ANCHOR_IN_WARMUP (HYPOTHESES until the real CSV is replayed)
Everything here was run on the Python model with seeded random walks (`tests/t003_explore_synthetic.py`) and constructed series. It shows what the PROPOSED rules do on artificial data; it does not show what gold does.
- FACT (model, synthetic): at the default parameters (L=300, R=1000) 29 of 60 random walks of 1400 bars are LIMITED; 20 of them with exactly the three reasons of the observation; ANCHOR_IN_WARMUP never appears alone (0 of 29): the anchor rule is not what makes the state unreliable on these series. At R=2000 on 3000 bars 16 of 30 are still LIMITED (7 with the three reasons). More depth does not remove the class.
- H1 (strongest, supported by synthetic evidence): the final state of replay A is a FROZEN HOLD / RECONFIRMED whose cause (the wick and the frozen level's pivots) lies before the first bar of replay B. The rules give a frozen state no end: it ends only by a body close beyond the frozen level (flip) or by a further wick (restart), never because price moved far away, and while frozen the new swings are ignored. So the state depends on bars as old as the frozen cause, and replay B (which starts 300 bars later) cannot see it. Evidence: the final state of A was frozen in 52 of 60 synthetic series (86 percent of the 100-series run); in 23 of the 29 LIMITED cases A's binding reference is older than B's first bar. Constructed series (`sc_old_frozen`, test group 27, SYNTHETIC): a wick-HOLD followed by a monotone decline is reliable while the HOLD is inside B and LIMITED once the tail grows by 100 bars, for every R tried (223, 323, 423), with A = BEAR/HOLD and B = NONE and reasons `AB_STATE_DIFF+ANCHOR_IN_WARMUP`. The depth needed grows with the age of the frozen state without bound, so trying ever larger depths cannot fix it. The existing synthetic case `p16d_limit_r140` (an old bull bias whose protecting low is far below price while a newer window seeds a bear) gives the same three reasons as the observation.
- H2: periods in which a bias exists with NO protecting level (P2/P3: no flip can fire without a level). On synthetic walks a mean of 14 percent (max 39) of the bars of LIMITED cases are in such runs; the causes named in the few runs I inspected (5) were "candidate already closed beyond" and "no bounded swing between the latest extreme and its predecessor" (why they arise, for example a candidate confirmed only after a quick rebound had already crossed it, is a plausible mechanism that I did not test). By the rules no flip can fire during such a run, so two windows that started with different seeds keep different biases (reasoning from the rules, not measured); the diagnostics show since which bar and why. In `p16e_event_only_diff` (synthetic) the level is undefined since the flip bar in both replays and the only difference is FLIP versus BREAK_SET.
- H3: eligible-FVG tracks of a long HOLD / SWEEP_WAIT are never dropped except by invalidation, so late `RECONFIRMED` / `RECONF_LOST` events refer to old FVGs that exist only in the longer window. On synthetic walks (R=1000, 60 series) 25 of the 29 LIMITED cases have an event difference in the zone; RECONF_LOST is on one side or is the first extra event in 12 of those 25; the other first differences are mostly a count difference whose first extra event is CONTINUATION, FLIP or INTERNAL_BREAK.
- H4 (anchor too conservative): not supported on synthetic data (see the first FACT). The anchor of a frozen state includes the previous-extreme pivot `lv_prev`, which can be older than the level itself; this matters only when the frozen state is already unstable.
- Not explained / not tested: swing identity near the window starts (the 2/2 pivots need bars on both sides); in the examples above the first differing bar of the state keys is B's own first bar, which is consistent with B simply starting with no history, not with a swing identity defect.
- NO implementation defect of the A/B check or the replay was identified: every LIMITED synthetic case examined is explained by the strategy rules as written (P3 frozen levels without expiry, P2 undefined levels, P7). They touch F5 of the task ("how long RECONFIRMED stays valid, whether the frozen level is re-derived"). Nothing was changed in the algorithm.
