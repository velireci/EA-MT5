## Correction after the Codex review of 43fc94a (relayed: Python 11/0; MetaEditor DailyLimitsTest 0 errors / 0 warnings; regression reproduced)

Regression: balance_start 1e-15, closed_result 0, no trades, max_trades 2 (set), valid day -> DAILY_LOSS_LIMIT and cancel_pending_due = true. Cause: the tolerance `4*eps*max(1, threshold)` has an absolute floor of 4*eps (8.9e-16), larger than the 2% threshold of such a balance (2e-17), so a zero result satisfied `closed_result <= -threshold + tolerance`.
Fix (Python and MQL5): the tolerance is `4*eps*threshold`, relative, always smaller than the threshold; the rule also requires `closed_result < 0`; a balance whose threshold would underflow (`balance_start / 50 < DBL_MIN`) is refused as INVALID_SNAPSHOT:balance_start. At normal balances the tolerance is the same as before (threshold >= 1), so equality and binary noise are treated as before.
Regressions: 14 new scenarios (zero, positive and 1e-30 results with balance 1e-15; a small loss below, at and beyond the line on a 1e-15 balance; balance 0.01 and 0.5; thresholds that underflow - balances 1e-307 and 3e-308 - are refused; 1.2e-306 is the smallest representable threshold and accepted; balance 1e300 with a zero result and with a loss at the line); a property test over 26 balances from 5e-324 to 1.7e308 x 7 zero/positive results plus 20000 random balances (10^-320..10^308): no daily-loss reason and no cancel flag ever; subnormal-threshold balances (5e-324 ... DBL_MIN) are refused; the tolerance is below the threshold for 8 balances while equality still blocks, a 1e-16 relative shortfall (1 ulp of noise) still blocks and a 1e-9 shortfall does not. Mutants: 21 (new: absolute tolerance floor restored - this is the reproduced defect; underflowing threshold accepted), all caught.
