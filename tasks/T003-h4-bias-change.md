## 2. Terms
- Closed bars only, chronological, as in T002. The forming bar is not read by the replay (the only exception is the restrictive overlay P12).
- Pivot time = open time of the pivot candle. Confirmation time = close of the 2nd bar to its right (T002). A swing, and anything derived from it, is unknown before its confirmation time. Marks at the pivot must show the confirmation time so they do not suggest earlier knowledge.
- Body break (P1): an H4 candle CLOSE strictly beyond the level (SELL context: close > S; BUY context: close < S). Equality is not a break.
- Wick only (P1): high > S (BUY: low < S) with the close not beyond S. "Level crossed without a confirmed change" means exactly this.
- A bar's events are known at that bar's close (open time + period).
- State known before bar t: the swings confirmed by the close of bar t-1 and the bars up to t-1. Levels, candidates and eligibility used to judge bar t come ONLY from this state; bar t is never used to decide whether a level exists.
- Wick record fields (P4): source_wick_time = open time of the bar whose high/low crossed the level (the physical wick); source_wick_known_time = its close; detected_at = the first close at which the machine could know that this is a wick against a held level = max(level known time, source_wick_known_time, start time of the bias); retroactive = (detected_at > source_wick_known_time).
- Candidate level: a level derived by P2/P10 from the state known before a bar.
- Consumed candidate: a candidate that a closed bar has already crossed and whose crossing event (wick or break) the state machine has processed.
- Protecting level: the consumed candidate kept FROZEN while the state is HOLD or RECONFIRMED; it is the reference for later wick/body tests. A consumed candidate is never selected again as a fresh candidate for the old bias, but the protecting level remains in force.

## 3. Proposals
P1. Body versus wick as in section 2. A body break takes precedence over wick-only for the same bar.

P2. Structural level (formalises "the relevant level"; still a proposal, not validated). SELL context: consider the confirmed, non-ambiguous swing lows in order. A "lower low" is strictly below the confirmed low immediately before it (equality is not lower). Take the latest lower low and the low before it. The structural high S is the HIGHEST confirmed swing high whose pivot lies strictly between them. Swing highs inside the leg that are not its top, and corrective higher lows after the last lower low, never become the level; the higher high before the leg is not used (S1). BUY context is the mirror: latest higher high, structural low S = LOWEST confirmed swing low strictly between that high and the high before it.
Timing: the candidate is derived from the state known before the evaluated bar t, never from a state that includes bar t. If bar t crosses the candidate, that is the event to process (P1/P4), not a reason to discard the level. A candidate is UNDEFINED ("no evidence") only if a bound or the high is missing, or if a closed bar between its pivot and t-1 already closed beyond it (a body break the machine handled earlier: a consumed candidate).
Crossings that happened before the level was known (retroactive): when a level is first derived for a bias at time t0 (the close of the bar at which both bounding swings are confirmed, or the start of the bias if later), the bars from its pivot up to t0 are examined. A body close beyond it rejects the candidate. Otherwise, if one or more wicks crossed it, the wick record keeps source_wick_time of the LATEST such wick and sets detected_at = t0 (retroactive = true); HOLD starts at detected_at, not at source_wick_time. The machine does not claim that entries were blocked before detected_at: the interval between source_wick_known_time and detected_at is reported as "wick before the level was known", and T003 trades nothing anyway. In state NONE (no bias) there is nothing to hold: the wick is stored on the candidate as "wicked before known" and changes no state; a later body close beyond the candidate is still a break (P10), and if a bias is later started on that candidate (seed at time t0') the same rule applies with detected_at = t0'.
The level is known from max(confirmation of S's pivot, confirmation of the lower low); only bars opening at or after that time can produce events against it as events of the machine.
Consumed candidate versus protecting level: after a wick at bar t the candidate is consumed AND kept as the frozen protecting level of HOLD; it is not re-derived and not subject to the "already crossed" rejection while HOLD or RECONFIRMED lasts.
Validation is by the visual comparison of section 10, not by a pass/fail claim. Internal liquidity (for example an interior low of the structure) is not the structural level; it is handled by P14.

P3. Bias layer with hysteresis next to T002's swing-based direction (unchanged, still displayed; the two may disagree). States NONE -> BIAS_BEARISH / BIAS_BULLISH (P10 says how the first bias is set). Once a bias exists, its direction can be reversed by ONE thing only: a body close (P1) beyond the CURRENT protecting level of that bias (the P2 level while ACTIVE, the frozen level during HOLD and RECONFIRMED), whatever T002's direction says, including MIXED. Other breaks are not flips: in a bullish bias a close above the latest confirmed higher high is a CONTINUATION event (no state change), and a close below an interior low that is not the protecting low is an INTERNAL_BREAK observation (P14, no state change); the bearish bias mirrors this. The multiple candidates of P10 do not apply while a bias exists. The level may be re-derived while ACTIVE (and, as a proposal, while SWEEP_WAIT); it is frozen during HOLD and during RECONFIRMED whatever the reason (STRUCT or INTERNAL), with the freeze and thaw moments of P14 (revision 7). After a flip the new bias has no level until P2 yields one; while it has none, no reversal can fire and other breaks are continuation/internal events; the panel shows "bias without level" next to T002's direction so a long counter-move without a level is visible.

P4. Wick event (closed-bar). A wick-only bar against the level moves ACTIVE -> HOLD. The wick record keeps SEPARATE timestamps: source_wick_time (the physical wick bar), source_wick_known_time (its close) and detected_at (when the machine could first know it is a wick against a held level; equal to source_wick_known_time when the level was known before the bar, later when the level was discovered afterwards, P2). HOLD starts at detected_at and new entries from the previous setup are blocked from detected_at (S3); nothing is claimed about the past before it. The structure is not cancelled: the bias stays (a wick is not a direction change, S2). In NONE no HOLD exists (P2).

P5. Eligible new FVG (S4), SELL: a bearish H4 FVG (T002 definition) whose candle A is the wick candle or later, so that candle C closes after the wick candle; no candle before the wick candle takes part. The timestamp that qualifies the new FVG is source_wick_time (the physical wick bar), not detected_at: candle A must have an open time at or after source_wick_time, so an FVG formed between the wick and a late detection is eligible by its timing. Touches count for reconfirmation only on bars that open at or after detected_at (the robot was not waiting before it, P6). The exact time definition of "new FVG after the wick" is still OPEN (section 8, F1); this is only the working interpretation. Pre-wick FVGs are marked PRE_WICK and are ineligible even if touched again. BUY: a bullish FVG, same timing (consistent with E3, which says the zone formed after the grab).

P6. Reconfirmation, per eligible FVG and on closed bars after its candle C that also open at or after detected_at. The touch is at the H4 FVG and counts at the CLOSE of the H4 candle that touched it (client information, S5). The state rules are T002's proposals: INVALID when a close is strictly beyond the far edge, TOUCHED when the wick range intersects the zone, INVALID wins on the same bar. A touched, still valid eligible FVG moves HOLD -> RECONFIRMED_H4 and records its ID. With several eligible FVGs, T002's nearest-first ordering decides which is shown first, and each can be reached in turn (client: the nearest first, then the next one).

P7. Interrupting transitions: a body break (HOLD or RECONFIRMED) flips the bias and discards the reconfirmation progress; another wick-only bar restarts HOLD from the LATEST wick (earlier eligible FVGs become PRE_WICK); the recorded FVG becoming INVALID returns RECONFIRMED -> HOLD; an eligible FVG becoming INVALID before its touch is dropped, and with none left HOLD continues. RECONFIRMED_H4 only means "H4 context ready, awaiting a new M15 structure break"; the M15 phase is not part of T003.

P8. BUY mirrors every rule. This is a proposal. The client's BUY example (E1-E6) is evidence for BUY of the sequence grab without a confirmed change -> new H4 FVG after the grab -> return/touch -> M15 structure break, with entry at the M15 FVG (S5); it does not confirm the other mirrored details (body-close rule for the flip, which FVG candle must follow the wick, hysteresis, the choice of the protected level).

P9. Live and historical share one pure replay over closed bars: `bars[0..n-1]` -> bias state, level, events with their times, eligible FVGs. Live applies it on init and on each new closed H4 bar; historical applies the same function to the bars closed by AsOfTime (T002's as-of filter). The replay is causal: state at bar t uses only swings confirmed by t and bars up to t. Window handling is in P11.

P10. Starting the bias FROM NONE only, with a defined precedence between the T002 seed and structural breaks. The several candidates below are used only while the state is NONE; as soon as a bias exists, P3 applies and they are ignored (only the current protecting level can flip a bias). The replay scans the bars chronologically from the start of its window; at each bar t while the state is NONE, in this order:
1. Break events of bar t against the structural levels known BEFORE t. Candidates of the up kind: the P2 level of a downward leg (S_high) and the latest confirmed higher high; of the down kind: the P2 level of an upward leg (S_low) and the latest confirmed lower low. A body close beyond a known candidate is a real break and sets the bias in that direction (origin BREAK). A level that was not yet known before t cannot be broken at t. If breaks of BOTH kinds happen on the same bar, the result is NONE with reason CONFLICT, and step 2 is NOT executed for this bar: the T002 seed never overwrites a CONFLICT in the same candle. The seed may apply again from the next bar.
2. Only if step 1 produced neither a bias nor a CONFLICT, the seed from T002: T002's direction evaluated on the prefix including bar t (BEARISH / BULLISH) sets the bias when its P2 level is defined (origin SEED_SWINGS). Evaluated at every bar, a trend T002 saw earlier is kept by hysteresis even when T002 shows MIXED at the evaluation time.
Precedence: a break (an observed event on a closed bar against a level known earlier) outranks the seed (a structure-derived expectation). If a break and the seed point in opposite directions on the same bar, the break decides and T002's direction is shown as a disagreement. If there is no usable break, no defined level and no seed, the result is NONE with reason NO_EVIDENCE. No direction is invented in any of these cases.
A window that starts mid-trend with T002 saying MIXED can therefore still rebuild a bias from the breaks it contains, with its own level, using no bar after the evaluation time.

P11. History, restart and window: ONE mechanism. It replaces the earlier mix of "replay the last REBUILD_BARS at every bar" and an "expand-only window"; expand-only windows and re-basing are dropped.
Mechanism: a STATELESS fixed-depth rebuild with a depth-sensitivity check. Nothing is stored between evaluations or restarts: on init and on every new closed H4 bar the state is rebuilt from closed bars only. Persistence is rejected for T003: a stored state would be a second source of truth that can be stale after offline gaps, a recompile, a manual chart change or revised history, and the platform storage mechanisms are unverified in this project; it could be added later only as a hint that the replay checks, as a separate decision.
Parameters (all PROPOSED inputs/constants, none specified by the client; defaults are working values):
- lookback L = InpLookbackBars (the T002 analysis window, default 300).
- REBUILD_BARS R: replay depth in closed H4 bars; default 1000; never below 3*L.
- WARM_UP W = L: the first W bars of a replay are replayed but no state or event derived mainly from them is trusted.
- CHECK_DEPTH D = W.
- HARD_CAP = 5000 closed bars: never replay more, whatever the inputs or the server offers (time and memory bound).
Evaluation: R_eff = min(R, HARD_CAP, bars available from the server).
1. Replay A over the last R_eff closed bars.
2. Replay B over the last R_eff - D closed bars (same final bar).
3. The result is RELIABLE only if all three hold: (i) R_eff >= 3*L (so that B still has a trusted region of at least L bars after its own warm-up); (ii) A and B agree on the final machine state (bias, origin, level pivot times and prices, the wick record including source_wick_time and detected_at, the eligible FVG ids and their states) and on every event dated inside B's trusted region (after B's warm-up); (iii) the dependency anchor of the state (the earliest bar it needs: for origin BREAK the earlier of the break bar and the level's pivots, minus the bars that confirm them; for HOLD/RECONFIRMED the earlier of the source wick bar (if any), the frozen level's pivots and, for an internal wait, the sweep bar; for SEED_SWINGS the seed's swings) lies after A's warm-up. Revision 6, technical proposal: "minus the bars that confirm them" means the LeftBars EXISTING bars before each reference. Each reference is located by its absolute open time in the chronological bar series (the full series the evaluation was given), the anchor is the absolute open time of the bar LeftBars positions earlier, and the comparison with the warm-up stays in absolute times. Weekends and holes in the history therefore never produce a time that is not a bar. If a reference is not found in the series, or fewer than LeftBars bars exist before it, the history is missing: the anchor is not invented, the report says "missing history: <reason>" and the state is LIMITED_BY_HISTORY with the reason ANCHOR_HISTORY_MISSING (no market event, direction UNKNOWN, as for any LIMITED state). An anchor inside the window is necessary and never sufficient: condition (ii) is what tests the sensitivity to depth.
4. Support flag reported with every state: CONVERGED (RELIABLE, origin BREAK or a HOLD/RECONFIRMED/SWEEP_WAIT), SEEDED (RELIABLE but origin SEED_SWINGS: weaker, may change with more history), LIMITED_BY_HISTORY (not RELIABLE).
What the flags mean and do not mean: CONVERGED means "A and B agree at depth R_eff against R_eff - D". It is evidence at that depth, NOT a guarantee that more history, or history older than R_eff, would not change the state; reports say "converged at depth R_eff", never "unchanged by additional history". A difference that appears only in history older than R_eff cannot be detected; this is a documented limit and the tests record it as a limitation, not as detected. A bias or HOLD whose origin is older than the window is not representable: the result is whatever the window supports, flagged only if A and B disagree or the anchor falls in the warm-up.
LIMITED_BY_HISTORY: the direction is reported UNKNOWN (limited by history); no bias, HOLD, flip, wick, sweep or reconfirmation is asserted; T002's own direction is still shown. No market event is emitted for it. Events are emitted only for the latest closed bar t, when the state before t and the state after t are both RELIABLE and A and B agree on that event. A transition into or out of LIMITED_BY_HISTORY, and any difference that arises only from depth (a restart with a different depth, growing history, the window sliding), is logged as a RE-DERIVATION with its cause and is never a FLIP, WICK, RECONFIRMED or SWEEP event.
Depth not reached: if the server (or a failed copy) gives fewer bars than R, R_eff is what is available and "reduced depth R_eff of R" is logged; if R_eff < 3*L the check cannot run and the state is LIMITED_BY_HISTORY (UNKNOWN). A copy that returns no bars follows T002's lifecycle (map cleared, no event). A requested depth above HARD_CAP is clamped to HARD_CAP and logged.
Sliding and restart: because the rebuild is stateless the window slides by one bar with each new bar; a state that depends on bars leaving the window changes the A/B comparison and shows as LIMITED_BY_HISTORY or as a RE-DERIVATION, never as an event. A restart is an ordinary evaluation: the same bars give the same state; there is no stored state to be stale.
Technical proposal (fail-safe for the later execution task, NOT a client statement, the owner's decision, F7): while the state is LIMITED_BY_HISTORY an execution module should block new entries. T003 only reports the state.

P12. Intrabar block, Codex's technical proposal (not client-confirmed, not implemented). In live mode, when the high (SELL) or low (BUY) of the FORMING H4 candle crosses the held level strictly, new entries are blocked immediately and temporarily. The overlay can only block: it never changes the bias, never starts a HOLD, never enables an entry, never produces a reconfirmation. The direction change is confirmed only after the H4 close beyond the level. At that close:
- wick-only (the bar crossed the level but did not close beyond it): the overlay becomes the closed-bar HOLD of P4 (the block continues; the HOLD is dated by the closed bar, the overlay start is logged as an observation time);
- body break (close beyond the level): the overlay ends in the bias flip of P3; no HOLD is entered for the old bias;
- a crossing that exactly touches the level (equality) is not a crossing.
The forming bar is read only by this overlay and only to restrict. Historical mode (closed bars only, no ticks) cannot reproduce the overlay and says so; live and historical therefore differ in timing by design. The overlay reads the forming bar's high/low from the series, not a tick feed.

P13. Target candidate for display (proposal; E6 and E5 are one example, no universal rule). In the trend direction the ERL candidate shown for comparison is the latest confirmed higher high (BUY) / lower low (SELL) as defined in P10, kept apart from local levels (for example a swing created by the pullback). It is displayed only; T002's ERL (interval based) is unchanged and both may be shown. Open details: whether the candidate must still be untaken, must lie beyond price, how it is chosen when several qualify, and the relation to the 2R policy (F5, F3). Compared with the client's example by the section 10 protocol, never claimed validated.

P14. Internal liquidity grab (technical proposal; the sequence is client-confirmed in E1-E6, the detection and the wait are NOT). Client-confirmed flow, BUY side: internal liquidity grab without a confirmed change of trend -> new H4 FVG after the grab -> return/touch -> M15 structure break (entry at the M15 FVG). The proposal must not be mixed up with a break of the protecting level.
- Identification (BUY; SELL by mirror): with the protecting low S_low defined (P2), the interior liquidity candidate L_int is the lowest confirmed swing low whose pivot is after the pivot of the latest higher high that defines S_low (the corrective lows of the pullback). An INTERNAL_SWEEP event is raised at closed bar t, using the state before t, when low_t < L_int strictly and close_t >= L_int and low_t >= S_low (the protecting low is not crossed). Without a defined S_low or L_int nothing is raised ("no evidence"). A sweep that crosses S_low is not internal: it is the structural wick/break of P1/P4.
- Transition: ACTIVE -> SWEEP_WAIT (s = t). The bias does not change and no level is frozen; SWEEP_WAIT keeps updating the level (proposal). An eligible FVG is a bullish H4 FVG whose candle A is the sweep candle or later (the same working interpretation as P5, F1). When an eligible FVG is touched and still valid at the close of the touching H4 candle (client information, S5), SWEEP_WAIT -> RECONFIRMED_H4 (reason INTERNAL, FVG id); the M15 phase is outside T003.
- Freeze, thaw and re-derivation (revision 7, TECHNICAL PROPOSAL, not a client statement; aligns the code with P3 and replaces the revision 6 rule, which re-derived one bar later). FREEZE: at the touch the protecting level KNOWN BEFORE the touching candle is frozen. The touching candle itself is not allowed to change it: a swing confirmed by that candle (for instance a new lower low whose pivot is two bars earlier) does not replace the event's level. NO LEVEL, NO RECONFIRMATION (AMBIGUITY 20a): a touch while no protecting level is defined (SWEEP_WAIT with the level rejected or missing) is NOT recorded: no RECONFIRMED event, nothing is frozen, the wait continues, and a later touch of a valid eligible FVG while a level is defined can record. THAW AT T: when the recorded FVG becomes INVALID on bar T, RECONFIRMED(INTERNAL) returns to SWEEP_WAIT (RECONF_LOST) and the level thaws on that bar; if the state stays SWEEP_WAIT the level is RE-DERIVED AT THE END OF T from the closed bars up to T and used from T+1 (bar T itself was judged against the level known before T). The continuation and interior candidates are kept through the freeze and refreshed by that derivation, so CONTINUATION, INTERNAL_BREAK and INTERNAL_SWEEP are raised normally on T+1. ANOTHER FVG ON T: if bar T also touches another valid eligible FVG, the state is RECONFIRMED(INTERNAL) again with that FVG, the level stays frozen at the one known before T, and nothing is derived on T. Priority is unchanged: a body break of the frozen level flips the bias, a wick through it starts HOLD (restart), both before any reconfirmation logic. A structural RECONFIRMED (from HOLD) never thaws: its loss returns to HOLD, which stays frozen.
- Precedence: while in SWEEP_WAIT the structural logic stays active. A wick through S_low moves to HOLD (P4) and a body break flips the bias (P1/P3); both supersede the wait. A further internal sweep restarts the wait from the latest sweep.
- Entries: whether the previous setup's new entries are blocked during SWEEP_WAIT is NOT stated by the client (S3 concerns the structural wick). The technical proposal is to block them, with a distinct reason code, for the owner/client to confirm (F8).
- What the proposal does not claim: it does not assume that the low grabbed in the client's picture is L_int or that it is not the protecting low; the classification is an observation of the section 10 comparison. A body close below L_int (an interior low) is neither an internal sweep nor a break of the protecting level here: it is recorded as an INTERNAL_BREAK observation with no state change, and what it should mean is open (F8).

Wick record: source_wick_time, source_wick_known_time, detected_at (section 2). HOLD starts at detected_at.

The level used for bar t is never recomputed with bar t. The intrabar overlay (P12) sits outside this table.

### 11.2 Why A and B stay frozen: the concrete transitions (code: Python `tests/t003_reference_test.py`, MQL5 `src/include/H4Bias.mqh`; read in this round, not changed)
Per closed bar, `process_bar` / `H3ProcessBar` runs `bias_step` (H3BiasStep), then `fvg_step` (H3FvgStep), then `derive_step` (H3DeriveStep); the state is `St` with the key fields `dir, phase, origin, none_reason, lv_id/lv_price (+ lv_pivot, lv_ext, lv_prev, lv_known), frozen, wick record (wk_src, wk_known, wk_det, wk_retro), sweep_time, rec_id, recon_reason` plus the FVG tracks; `state_key` joins BIAS, PH, ORG, NR, LV, FRZ, WK, SW, REC, RSN and EL (tracks with FRESH/RECORDED/INVALID). With a bias in HOLD or RECONFIRMED:
1. `derive_step` returns at once (`if st.dir == 0 or st.frozen: return`): no new swing is used for the level, the latest HH/LL (`ex_*`) or the interior candidate (`li_*`).
2. `bias_step` returns after the flip/wick tests (`if ph != ACTIVE and ph != SWEEP_WAIT: return`): CONTINUATION, INTERNAL_BREAK and INTERNAL_SWEEP are never raised while frozen.
3. The ONLY exits from a frozen HOLD or RECONFIRMED are: (a) FLIP, `do_flip`: a CLOSE strictly beyond the frozen level; (b) a wick-only bar against the same frozen level: `start_hold` again (restart; new wick record, tracks rebuilt, `rec_id` cleared), level unchanged; (c) in `fvg_step`, RECONFIRMED to HOLD (STRUCT) or SWEEP_WAIT (INTERNAL) when the recorded FVG becomes INVALID (`RECONF_LOST`). Only (c) with INTERNAL clears `frozen`. A structural RECONFIRMED returns to HOLD, and HOLD keeps `frozen = True` (set in `start_hold`); a structural freeze has no thaw.
4. An FVG track is dropped only by invalidation (a close beyond its far edge); pre-wick FVGs are PRE_WICK for good.
5. Consequence: a flip needs a close beyond ONE price, the level frozen at the wick. The synthetic scenario `SC-1` (scratch, earlier round) shows it: a bear structure, a wick above the level (HOLD), a decline, then a rebuilt HH/HL structure (T002 says BULLISH) with every close and every high below the frozen level: bias stays BEAR/HOLD, level frozen, no event after the WICK. A window that starts after the wick seeds BULL from the same bars.
6. With the relayed facts: A is BULL at a frozen low far below the later prices; B is BEAR at a frozen high far above the later prices. Each state is "valid" by the written rules for its whole window and neither window contains the single event that would reconcile them. Whether the real market did this is for the private-case analysis, not asserted here.
7. Where `origin` is read (checked in this round, Python and MQL5 agree): it is SET in `none_step` (BREAK or SEED) and in `do_flip` (BREAK). It is READ only (i) by `state_key` (field ORG, hence AB_STATE_DIFF), (ii) by `evaluate` to choose the flag SEEDED instead of CONVERGED (`origin == SEED and not frozen`), (iii) by the diagnostics text. No decision of `none_step`, `bias_step`, `fvg_step` or `derive_step` reads the NAME. `origin_ref` is read only by `anchor_refs_named` (unfrozen states), i.e. by the ANCHOR_IN_WARMUP / ANCHOR_HISTORY_MISSING reliability check. `bias_start` (close of the start/flip bar) IS read by `derive_step` (retroactive wick: `det = max(lvl.known, wk_known, bias_start)`), so it can change the future (see 11.8).

### 11.3 Two layers, and two different things called "structure break" (T)
(a) STRUCTURE layer = what the H4 market is doing now.
- State: `bias` (NONE/BULL/BEAR), protecting level PL (id, price, pivot/ext/prev times, known time), pending candidates (latest HH/LL `ex`, interior `li`, with their consumed flags), `origin` and `bias_start` (see 11.8). Identity by absolute times only (the level id already is `SL|SH <pivot>_<ext>_<prev>`).
- It runs on EVERY closed bar in every setup phase. It is not frozen by a wick, HOLD, SWEEP_WAIT or RECONFIRMED. There is no `frozen` flag for the structure.
- Flip: unchanged single trigger (P3): a body close strictly beyond the CURRENT PL (11.4).

TWO events that must not be confused (SELL naming; BUY by mirror). P = the H4 period. All times are open times of bars unless called "close".
| | (a) CONFIRMATION of a new swing LL (HH for BUY) | (b) BOS = a close beyond a level KNOWN BEFORE the breaking candle |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | A new confirmed swing low, strictly below the previous confirmed swing low. A statement about KNOWLEDGE of a swing (2/2 pivot as in T002). | A closing break, in the trend direction, of a level that already existed as known. |
| Conditions | pivot bar p is a swing low with 2 bars on each side (T002 comparison, unchanged); its price is strictly below the previous confirmed swing low. No condition on any close. A wick pierce is enough. | Level L (price, `known_time`); breaking bar b with `open(b) >= L.known_time` (L was known before b began); `close(b)` strictly below L.price (equality is not a break); no earlier bar j with `pivot_time(L) < open(j) < open(b)` closed beyond L (the FIRST closing break only). A bar that only reaches below L with its low (close not beyond) is a wick, not a BOS (S2). |
| Timestamps | `pivot_time = open(p)`; `confirm_time = open(p) + 3P` (= the close of the 2nd bar to its right = `open(p+2) + P`); first bar that may use it: the bar opening at `confirm_time`. | `L.known_time` (for a swing level: its `confirm_time`); `open(b)`; `close(b) = open(b) + P` = the time at which the BOS is known. |
| Known at | `confirm_time`. Before that the swing does not exist for the machine. | `close(b)`. |
| Not this | It is not a BOS: it can exist without any close below the old low. | A close below a level that became known only AFTER `open(b)` (the 2/2 confirmation arrives later) is not a BOS of b; it is a retroactive crossing, handled by the existing P2 retroactive rule (unchanged). |
The two are independent. The BOS bar b is normally the bar that closes below the old low; the new LL pivot is the lowest low of the move and is confirmed 2 bars after its pivot, so `confirm_time` of the new LL is usually LATER than `close(b)`; a new LL can also be confirmed without any BOS (a wick pierce below the old low with no close below it).
The P2 candidate (UNCHANGED selection algorithm) is therefore NOT called a "confirmed BOS". It is "a protecting-level candidate built on a CONFIRMED new extreme (a)". Level timestamps for the PL: `pivot_time` (the counter-swing high S), `ext_time` (the new LL pivot), `prev_time` (the LL before it), `known_time = max(confirm_time(S), confirm_time(new LL))`; acceptance is evaluated at the end of the bar whose close equals `known_time` and the PL is used from the bar that opens at `known_time`.
WHICH of the two the PL update depends on (PROPOSAL, awaiting the client answer, 11.9): the default update rule depends on (a) only, plus the rejection rule (iv) "no close beyond the candidate". It does NOT require a BOS (b). The alternative U2 additionally requires a BOS (b) of the previous low by a bar b with `L_prev.known_time <= open(b)` and `close(b) <= confirm_time(new LL)`; kept as the validation alternative of question C5. For diagnostics the implementation may report `bos_before` (true/false) on the accepted update; reported, not used by the default.

PL update ("ratchet", PROPOSAL AWAITING THE CLIENT ANSWER; selection algorithm P2 UNCHANGED): the P2/`derive_level` candidate is accepted as the new PL at the end of closed bar t only if (i) it comes from confirmed 2/2 swings known at t; (ii) its extreme pivot (the new LL/HH) is LATER than the extreme pivot of the current PL; (iii) it is strictly more advanced in the trend direction (bear: lower price; bull: higher price; equal keeps the current id); (iv) no closed bar between its pivot and t closed beyond it. Rule (iii) is the ratchet: the PL never retreats against the trend, so a wick top above the old PL cannot raise it. Used from bar t+1. A wick-only bar against the current PL is still the structural WICK (P4) and creates/replaces the setup; a body close flips the bias and ends the setup.
- ALTERNATIVES: R0, plain re-derivation without ratchet (rejected: the wick top can become the "highest high of the leg" and raise the trigger, see example 2 in 11.4; this can contradict S2). U2 (strict BOS first) as above. Time-based expiry, "price moved K ranges" (rejected: arbitrary). Persisting the frozen cause (rejected in P11). Shortening the freeze by the A/B window (rejected: tuning to the check).
- What could break: P10 (only the NONE start; unchanged); CONFLICT (unchanged); a crossed candidate leaves the old PL in force rather than "undefined" (inside setup phases only); a PL that stays old when no newer leg forms (hysteresis, intrinsic).
- If the client answers that the OLD level stays: the PL is not moved, the snapshot and the live PL coincide for the life of the bias, the structure is frozen again in effect, and the dependence on old wicks that LIMITED_BY_HISTORY shows would be a property of the client's rule, to be handled by a documented limit or another owner decision (nothing proposed here).

### 11.4 CURRENT level versus SNAPSHOT: one priority (T, PROPOSAL)
Definitions. CURRENT level = the structure's PL at the moment of the bar being judged (the one known before that bar). SNAPSHOT = the PL id and price AT THE MOMENT the setup was created, stored in the setup.
Single priority (chosen): the CURRENT level of the STRUCTURE decides direction change (FLIP). The snapshot is a REFERENCE ONLY: it records the context at setup creation (for the M15 interface, for display, for `setup_id`). It has NO independent invalidation role: a close beyond the snapshot that is not beyond the current level changes no state and ends no setup; it is reported as the diagnostic `SNAPSHOT_CROSSED` (provenance, not compared by the A/B check). Alternative considered and not chosen: the snapshot as an independent invalidation (two triggers; contradicts the single-trigger rule P3 and creates states where the structure says BEAR and the setup says ended).
Relation (a consequence of the ratchet, not an identity): with rule (iii) the current PL is never farther than the snapshot in the adverse direction, so a close beyond the snapshot is also beyond the current PL at the same bar or the flip happened earlier. This is NOT claimed as "the same event" in general: the FLIP is the structure's event; "snapshot crossed" is a diagnostic. Without the ratchet (R0) or if the client's answer changes the update rule, the relation must be re-examined.
Example 1 (SYNTHETIC, generic units; SELL; scratchpad mini-engine `r9_examples.py`, NOT the product, not run against the model tests, FVG tracks not simulated: the flip ends the setup whatever its phase). Bars are H4; values (high/low/close). Bias BEAR is given; PL1 = 117 (swing high of bar 10) known since the close of bar 17. Swings use the simple 2/2 rule of the scratch engine.

### 11.5 SETUP/CONTEXT layer: three separate things, and when `setup_id` is kept (T; the supersession choice is UNVALIDATED)
State: `setup_id`, `phase` (NONE, HOLD, SWEEP_WAIT, RECONFIRMED), snapshot (PL id and price at creation; reference only, 11.4), wick record (source/known/detected/retro) or sweep record, eligible FVG tracks, recorded FVG id and reason (STRUCT/INTERNAL), `wait_start`. The revision 7 freeze/thaw disappears: nothing in the setup freezes the structure.
`setup_id` = `<type>@<source bar open time>#<snapshot PL id>` where type is WICK or SWEEP and the source bar is the physical wick/sweep bar (`wk_src` / `sweep_time`), NOT the detection time and not a window index, so a retroactive detection by another window gives the same id (the detection time depends on `bias_start`, the source bar does not).
The three separate things:
(i) LOSS OF THE FVG CONFIRMATION = event `RECONF_LOST`: the recorded FVG is invalidated (close beyond its far edge). It is NOT the end of the setup.
(ii) RETURN TO WAITING (consequence of (i), same bar): STRUCT reason: RECONFIRMED to HOLD; INTERNAL reason: RECONFIRMED to SWEEP_WAIT. KEPT: `setup_id`, snapshot, the wick or sweep record, `src_time`, `wait_start`, all other tracks that are not invalid (INVALID ones stay INVALID, pre-wick ones stay PRE_WICK). CLEARED: `rec_id`, `recon_reason`, the recorded flag of the lost track. A later touch of another valid eligible FVG gives RECONFIRMED again in the SAME setup (same `setup_id`, a different recorded FVG id). Entries stay blocked in HOLD (S3) and SWEEP_WAIT (F8, technical).
(iii) FINAL END = event `SETUP_ENDED(reason)`, phase NONE, setup_id retired for good. Reasons: `INVALIDATED_BY_FLIP` (the structure flipped, 11.4); `REPLACED` (a new wick or sweep created another setup; the event carries the new `setup_id`); `SUPERSEDED_BY_STRUCTURE` (variant B, UNVALIDATED proposal, below); `M15_OUTCOME` (reported by the M15 module, not implemented). `LIMITED_BY_HISTORY` is never an end reason: it makes the state unreliable and emits no market event.
Table: `setup_id` kept or new.
| Situation | `setup_id` | Previous setup | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| RECONFIRMED reached (touch at close of touching candle) | kept | continues | `recorded FVG id` set |
| RECONF_LOST (STRUCT or INTERNAL) | kept | continues, phase HOLD / SWEEP_WAIT | not an end |
| another eligible FVG forms / is invalidated / touched | kept | continues | tracks change only |
| structural wick-only bar against the CURRENT PL while a setup exists (restart, any phase) | NEW (new source bar) | ENDED, REPLACED | today's "restart"; tracks rebuilt, record replaced |
| internal sweep (ACTIVE or SWEEP_WAIT) | NEW | previous ended REPLACED if there was one | open point below: sweeps in HOLD/RECONFIRMED |
| structural wick while in SWEEP_WAIT (different source type) | NEW | ENDED, REPLACED | |
| retroactive wick when a PL is first accepted | NEW (source = the old wick bar) | none existed | same id for any window that finds the same wick |
| FLIP | none | ENDED, INVALIDATED_BY_FLIP | new bias has no setup; a later wick creates a new id |
| supersession (variant B only) | none | ENDED, SUPERSEDED_BY_STRUCTURE | see below |
| M15 outcome | none | ENDED, M15_OUTCOME | not implemented |
| LIMITED_BY_HISTORY, GAP, restart of the program | no id published while unreliable; the rebuild gives the same id (absolute times) | no end event | |
| after any SETUP_ENDED | a later setup always gets a NEW id; a retired id is never reused | | |
Supersession (UNVALIDATED proposal, C2; I do not validate it): variant B: when the structure PL advances (a newer leg accepted) while a setup born under an older PL exists, the setup ends with `SUPERSEDED_BY_STRUCTURE`. Variant A (default alternative): no supersession; the setup lives until a new wick/sweep, a flip, or the M15 outcome. Neither uses a bar count. B bounds the setup by market structure and removes the last unbounded dependency on an old wick; A keeps trades possible after a fast continuation. Under A a window that starts after the wick has no setup (AB_SETUP_DIFF would stay: a real, documented limit). The choice affects trading and is asked of the client (C2).
Open point (T, default = today's behaviour): today `bias_step` raises no INTERNAL_SWEEP while the phase is HOLD or RECONFIRMED. Because the structure now runs in every phase, the proposal keeps that: the observation events CONTINUATION and INTERNAL_BREAK may be raised in every phase (they change no state), but a new INTERNAL_SWEEP setup is created only from ACTIVE or SWEEP_WAIT. Whether a sweep should replace a HOLD/RECONFIRMED setup is not decided here and is not a client question of this round.
Transitions (all closed-bar): CREATE (structural wick, internal sweep, retroactive wick); REPLACE; RECONFIRM (S5, unchanged); RECONF_LOST then return to waiting; END as above. Entries are blocked in HOLD (S3, client) and SWEEP_WAIT (F8, technical); RECONFIRMED means "H4 context ready".

### 11.6 Interface to the NEXT (M15) module for a RECONFIRMED setup (proposal; M15 is out of scope; no entry is simulated)
H4 provides, read-only, once per closed H4 bar and on setup change: `setup_id`; `direction`; `reason` (STRUCT / INTERNAL); `ready` (true only in RECONFIRMED); snapshot level (id, price, known time; REFERENCE only); `live PL` (id, price; this is the invalidation reference); recorded FVG (id, bounds, candles A/B/C times, state, valid-from = the close of the touching candle); touch bar; valid-from (the later of the touch close and detected_at); the state of the other eligible FVGs; blocked flags (HOLD/SWEEP_WAIT).
Events the M15 module must observe (closed H4 bars; from the H4 module, not recomputed): `RECONF_LOST` (setup continues, `ready` becomes false, the outstanding M15 state keyed on (`setup_id`, recorded FVG id) must be dropped; a later RECONFIRMED on the same `setup_id` brings a new recorded FVG id); `SETUP_ENDED(reason)` with reasons INVALIDATED_BY_FLIP, REPLACED, SUPERSEDED_BY_STRUCTURE (variant B), M15_OUTCOME (the final end: no further H4 context for that id); `LIMITED_BY_HISTORY` (state unreliable: no new setup).
M15 returns to H4 only an outcome for the `setup_id`: NONE_YET / TRIGGERED / EXPIRED_BY_M15 / FAILED. TRIGGERED or FAILED ends the setup (M15_OUTCOME); what M15 considers an outcome is NOT defined here (F2/S6). Identity: `setup_id` plus the absolute FVG id; no window index.

What stays is the purpose: the check must still detect a real history dependence. The comparison is defined by FUNCTION: a field is compared if it can change the FUTURE behaviour (future decisions or future events).

A STRUCTURE difference does not hide the setup comparison.
Events compared and period: the comparison zone is unchanged in principle: after BOTH warm-ups, i.e. events dated at or after B's trusted start (B's warm-up end), which is later than A's; events are identified by ABSOLUTE bar open time, type and subject id (level id, FVG id or `setup_id`), with the resulting direction/phase for FLIP, SETUP_ENDED (reason) and RECONFIRMED (reason). Acceptance criterion (not a suggestion): the comparison must NOT be weakened merely so that the owner's real CSV yields a direction. The real CSV may remain LIMITED_BY_HISTORY or UNKNOWN after the change; that is an acceptable, reported outcome. Every field removed from the comparison must be listed in the table with the reason it cannot change the future; the table above is the starting list and is itself reviewed by Codex.

### 11.8 ORIGIN (SEED versus BREAK): provenance or function? (T)
Which case it is TODAY (code reading, 11.2 item 7): the NAME of the origin is provenance plus a reliability label (SEEDED vs CONVERGED); no decision reads it. It nevertheless enters `state_key`, so a name difference alone gives AB_STATE_DIFF today (observed in the synthetic AB-3). `origin_ref` feeds only the anchor reliability check. What DOES affect the future is `bias_start`: `derive_step` uses it in `det = max(lvl.known, wk_known, bias_start)` for a retroactive wick, which sets `wk_det` and `wait_start` (which touches count). A level accepted at a later bar is accepted at its `known_time` (11.3), which is after `bias_start`, so for it the maximum is decided by `known` or `wk_known`; `bias_start` can therefore matter only for the FIRST PL of a bias (a level known before the bias began and already pierced by a wick), i.e. while the bias has no PL. This is a code-reading expectation to be confirmed by R20 and a property test, not a proven fact.
Proposal: (1) a difference of the origin NAME alone is NOT a functional mismatch (diagnostic `ORG_DIFF`); (2) the SEEDED flag stays (A's own origin); (3) `bias_start` is compared only while the bias has no PL and no setup yet derived from it; afterwards it is provenance; (4) `origin_ref` stays in the anchor check; (5) the P10 precedence (break outranks seed, CONFLICT not overwritten the same bar) acts at the start decision and its effect is already in `bias`/`bias_start`; no separate comparison. Whether point (3) is exactly true for the final implementation is verified by regression case R20 (a counter-example would put `bias_start` back into the compared set).
Regression cases (described only, not coded): R19 origin differs, future behaviour identical: window A started by a break (BREAK), window B (starting later) seeded (SEED) the same direction; same PL, same pending candidates, same setup or none, same events in the zone; expected CONVERGED (A origin BREAK) with `ORG_DIFF` in the diagnostics, no AB_* reason, reliable. R20 origin differs and the future differs: B seeds at its start with no PL; the first PL of the bias was known before B's `bias_start` and was pierced by a wick in between; A (break earlier) has `wk_det`/`wait_start` earlier than B; a touch on a bar between the two times counts in A and not in B; expected `AB_SETUP_DIFF` (and AB_EVENT_DIFF if the touch falls in the zone), LIMITED_BY_HISTORY, and the diagnostics name `bias_start` as the differing future-affecting field, not the origin name.

### 11.9 Mechanical criterion and evidence for updating the protecting level (TECHNICAL, but the update itself AWAITS THE CLIENT ANSWER already requested)
At closed bar t, bias d, current PL (or none): derive the P2 candidate from the swings confirmed by t (2/2, `confirm_time` as in 11.3 (a)) with the unchanged `derive_level`; accept it per (i)-(iv). Evidence: (1) two successive extremes in the trend direction, the later strictly beyond the earlier; (2) a bounded counter-swing strictly between their pivots, the most adverse one, ties to the earliest; (3) `known_time` = the later of the two confirmations; (4) closes between the pivot and t. Ties: equal prices keep the current id; no bounded counter-swing means no candidate (the PL stays); a candidate crossed by a close is rejected. Not decided: whether a BOS (b) must precede the update (U2/C5); the P2 selection itself (unchanged). The dependence on (a) versus (b) is stated in 11.3.

### 11.10 Synthetic regression cases (to be described only; NOT coded; none has been run; generic, parametrised, no real prices or dates)
Parameters: leg size, number of legs, tail length, window start offset relative to the wick, lookback; SELL with BUY mirror.
R1. Setup does not freeze the structure: wick, then N further bearish legs with lower highs; the PL advances to the newer level; setup phase per variant (A stays; B ACTIVE). Same PL for every window start after the first leg.
R2. Ratchet: a wick top above the old PL never raises the PL; a candidate less advanced than the PL (also equal price) is not accepted.
R3. Flip by the live PL: a close above the newer PL but below the old one flips (example 1); a close above neither does not.
R4. No retreat after a crossed candidate: a candidate crossed by a close is not accepted and the old PL stays in force.
R5. Structural wick and internal sweep still create/replace the setup against the CURRENT PL; the snapshot is stored with the setup.
R6. Setup end reasons: INVALIDATED_BY_FLIP, REPLACED, SUPERSEDED_BY_STRUCTURE (B only), M15_OUTCOME (interface only); each event once, none by bar count; a long quiet tail never ends a setup. RECONF_LOST is NOT among them.
R7. Reconfirmation unchanged: pre-wick FVG ineligible; touch counted at the close of the touching candle.
R8. Window start sweep: the final STRUCTURE state for all starts after a chosen distance is the same; a case with NO newer leg (SC-1 type) is recorded as a documented limit, not as a pass.
R9. As-of: bars after the evaluation time replaced by garbage give the same result.
R10. Determinism, ids by absolute times only, restart gives the same state.
R11. Swing confirmation timing (11.3 a): the bar whose close equals `confirm_time` (the 2nd bar to the right) is judged with the state known before it and does not use the new LL; the bar opening at `confirm_time` does; `pivot_time` and `confirm_time` are reported; a PL candidate depending on it is not accepted earlier.
R12. BOS versus confirmation (11.3 b): (1) a wick-only pierce that becomes a confirmed new LL: (a) yes, BOS no; the default update accepts the candidate, the U2 variant does not; (2) a close below a low that is confirmed only after the breaking bar's open is not a BOS of that bar; (3) the first closing break only; equality is not a break.
R13. Current versus snapshot, example 1: the current PL updated and broken before the snapshot; FLIP at the bar, snapshot not crossed, setup ended INVALIDATED_BY_FLIP.
